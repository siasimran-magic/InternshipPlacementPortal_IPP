## 📸 2. Feature Walkthrough & Screenshots

### A. Opportunity Discovery & Job Board
Students browse verified placement drives and internships with live countdowns, CGPA cutoffs, and CTC details.
* `screenshots/demo_opportunity_board.png`

### B. Kanban Application Lifecycle Tracker
Visual drag-and-drop tracker categorizing applications from `Applied` to `Offer`.
* `screenshots/demo_application_kanban.png`

### C. Live Timed Aptitude Exam Simulator
Interactive test interface with synchronized countdown timer, anti-cheat detection, and auto-submit.
* `screenshots/demo_aptitude_simulator.png`

### D. Employability Readiness Analytics & Radar Chart
Personalized student analytics dashboard displaying percentile ranking, domain-wise radar visualization, and target revision recommendations.
* `screenshots/demo_analytics_radar.png`

### E. TPO Placement Drive Management Console
Training & Placement Officer administrative interface to publish drives, filter eligible candidate rosters, and broadcast notifications.
* `screenshots/demo_tpo_management.png`
